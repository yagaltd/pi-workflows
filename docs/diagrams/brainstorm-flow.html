/brainstorm — divergent research loop

Divergent thinking with a living research ledger. Evidence validates or kills branches; the human decides when to graduate. No code, no specs, no commitment.

Evidence only

A branch changes status ❓→✅/❌/💤 only on evidence from a research round. Never on opinion.

One ledger

.workflows/research/<slug>/ledger.md is the memory. Markdown tree; the chat is not the record.

Human graduates

The loop never self-exits. You pick /explore, /idea, park, or kill — reports ride along as context.
